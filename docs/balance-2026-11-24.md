# Balance — 24 November 2026

Only the requested five minigames change. Task generation, time limits, controls,
completion conditions, maximum scores and total competition cap stay unchanged.
The browser and server replay use the same scoring functions and sealing model.

## Accurate completion versus elapsed time

| Time used | Packing / WMS / Quality before | After | Picking before | After |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0% | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| 25% | 100 | 97 | 100 | 98 |
| 50% | 100 | 90 | 100 | 91 |
| 75% | 93 | 82 | 100 | 85 |
| 100% | 85 | 75 | 90 | 79 |

Packing, WMS Detective and Quality Control use 75 accuracy points plus 25 speed
points. Full speed points remain available in the first 15% of the time limit;
after that the speed portion decreases linearly. Other standard/classification
games retain their old 85/15 split and first-half grace period.

Speed Picking uses progress exponent 0.45 instead of 0.55, giving more credit for
individual correct picks. One early correct pick earns 32 rather than 25 points;
five early picks earn 66 rather than 61. After the initial 15% grace period, the
progress portion loses at most 12%, while the completion bonus also declines.
Five correct picks at timeout still earn 58 points. The four-point mistake penalty
is unchanged. These samples assume no wrong picks.

Pallet Sealing's excess-use penalty decreases from 6 to 4 points per waste unit
(one third less). Two waste units on a sealed pallet now leave 92 instead of 88
points. Layer accumulation, the excess threshold, coverage and completion do not
change. Torn seal still costs 12 points per break; outside wrapping costs 10 per
unit. Thus excess remains visible and penalized without the previous severity.

Conveyor Rush, Truck Loading, Memory Challenge and Final Dispatch keep their
existing grading. Server replay ignores client score claims and applies the new
rules itself. Deploy and restart the Python/Node backend along with the updated
frontend to activate the same rules on both sides.

## Later Speed Picking update: per-hit rewards

This section supersedes the earlier Speed Picking curve above; all other games
keep that balance. The old global countdown multiplier made five incomplete
picks always worth 58 points at timeout, regardless of when they were made.

Speed Picking now earns `85 × (correct / 10)^0.3` for progress and up to two
pace points per correct pick: `2 × exp(-responseSeconds / 2.5)`. Response time
starts at stage start or the previous correct pick, so wrong attempts do not
restart its clock. Wrong picks still cost four points; totals remain 0–100.
Earned pace points are retained through timeout. The normal browser measures
elapsed stage time; server replay derives the same times from validated pick
events, never from uploaded bonuses or a final score. Test-only STOP TIME
freezes the elapsed scoring clock along with its countdown.

Examples without mistakes:

| Correct boxes over the full 15 seconds, evenly spaced | Score |
|---|---:|
| 3 | 60 |
| 4 | 66 |
| 5 | 72 |
| 6 | 77 |
| 7 | 82 |
| 8 | 87 |
| 9 | 92 |
| 10 | 96 |

Five picks at 3/6/9/12/15 seconds earn 72; at 1/2/3/4/15 seconds earn 74;
at 1/2/3/4/5 seconds earn 76. Fast five picks spaced 0.6 seconds apart earn
77. Thus even equal counts and the same final-hit time can yield different
results, and an unfinished run can exceed the former 58-point plateau.
Very fast complete runs can still reach 100. Tasks, controls, duration,
round count and every other game's points remain unchanged.
